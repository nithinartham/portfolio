About me

Engineering content platforms for people and teams.

I specialize in Adobe Experience Manager as a Cloud Service and AEM 6.5, combining backend platform development with modern frontend engineering. My work includes reusable components, templates, dialogs, Sling Models, OSGi services, Content Fragments, Experience Fragments, Adobe Target personalization, and enterprise integrations.

I care deeply about accessibility, performance, maintainability, and author experience. I work closely with product, design, content, and engineering teams to turn complex requirements into dependable digital experiences.

Why I chose UI

↗
“A great interface makes technical work visible, useful, and easy to share—no matter how far apart people are.”

Living in the United States while my mother is in India helped me appreciate the reach of the web. As a UI developer, I can send a simple link and let her see and experience something I created.

That immediacy is what drew me to frontend engineering. My work does not remain hidden behind a system—it becomes an experience that family, teammates, and users around the world can interact with. For me, UI development is where engineering becomes visible and ideas become real.
